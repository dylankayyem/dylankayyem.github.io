  • FlowProof SEP 2026 - PRESENT

    FlowProof is a multi-tenant SaaS platform I'm building to monitor the business outcomes of automated workflows, not just whether they ran. Connected systems post events to a versioned ingestion API with API-key auth, idempotency, and rate limiting; a background worker correlates them into traces, evaluates assertions against what should have happened, and raises incidents with notifications when reality doesn't match. It's a TypeScript monorepo with a Next.js dashboard, a Fastify worker, PostgreSQL through Drizzle (including a Postgres-backed job queue), Clerk organizations as tenants, and Zod validation at every boundary. The core monitoring lifecycle is complete; integrations and billing are next.

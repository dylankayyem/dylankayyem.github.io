About me

Designer turned engineer, now building marketing technology.

Hi, thanks for checking out my page! I'm Dylan Kayyem, a UI/UX designer turned software engineer based in Broomfield, Colorado.

I'm currently the Junior Marketing Technology Manager for Titan Vans and Timber Van Kits, sister brands in Boulder that build custom camper vans and DIY van conversion kits. I own the marketing tech stack across both brands, bridging design, data, and engineering to connect them with their customers.

I leverage experience across UX design, software development, and marketing technology to build innovative and impactful products, and I'm committed to applying analytical skills and problem-solving to drive technological advancements.
